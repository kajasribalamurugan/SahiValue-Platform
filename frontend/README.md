\# SAHI VALUE



\### Kabaad ka Sahi Value



SAHI VALUE is a digital platform that connects informal e-waste collectors with authorized recyclers to make e-waste transactions more transparent, traceable, and accessible.



\## Problem



Informal e-waste collectors often face challenges such as:



\- Lack of reliable price information

\- Difficulty finding authorized recyclers

\- Limited transaction records and receipts

\- Unclear payment calculation

\- Unsafe e-waste handling practices

\- Language barriers when using digital platforms



\## Solution



SAHI VALUE provides a simple digital workflow connecting collectors and recyclers.



\### Collector



\- Add e-waste

\- Select material category

\- Enter declared weight

\- View estimated value

\- Find suitable recyclers

\- Track lot status

\- View verified earnings

\- Access digital receipts

\- Access safety guidance



\### Recycler



\- View incoming e-waste lots

\- Verify physical weight

\- Confirm handover

\- Manage purchases

\- Manage material rates

\- Track transactions

\- Generate digital receipts



\## Core Transaction Rule



The platform maintains two different weights:



\*\*Declared Weight\*\*  

The estimated weight entered by the collector.



\*\*Verified Weight\*\*  

The actual physical weight entered by the recycler during handover.



The final payment is calculated \*\*only using the Verified Weight\*\*.



\### Example



| Detail | Value |

|---|---:|

| Declared Weight | 20 kg |

| Verified Weight | 10 kg |

| Rate | ₹450/kg |

| Final Payment | ₹4,500 |



```text

Verified Weight × Rate

10 kg × ₹450

= ₹4,500

This ensures that the final settlement is based on the physically verified quantity.
